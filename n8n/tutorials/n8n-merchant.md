# How to hold a catalog export in n8n when a Merchant Center SKU has an invalid price

Use one offer to reproduce the hold, fix the price in the source and submit a new export. Match delivered offer IDs before the catalog team approves the upload.

1. Import [Merchant](../workflows/n8n-merchant.json) and follow the [connection guide](n8n-setup.md). Start with the [one-offer fixture](../fixtures/merchant-observation.json). One delivered audit can be charged even on a Free account.
2. On an export-complete event pass one item with `sequence`, `expectedOffers` and `input`. Obtain `expectedOffers` from the completed export's count of **distinct offer IDs**. Supply exactly one of `input.offers`, `input.feedXml` or `input.feedUrl`. For an authorized public HTTPS XML export on a Paid account use `feedUrl` and `authorizedFetch: true`. Prefer an immutable export URL or capture inline XML. Set `input.maxResults` at least as large as the expected distinct count.
3. Free supports up to 25 inline offers per run. Professional use supports up to 5,000 and authorized URL acquisition. For a full daily catalog choose an eligible account with one budget covering the complete export. At $0.90 per 1,000 delivered offer audits and $0.00005 for a default-memory start, 1,000 cost $0.90005 and 5,000 cost $4.50005. Example caps are $0.91 and $4.51 respectively. Verify the price and your remaining credit before enabling production.
4. Under **Review or preview**, `HOLD_EXPORT` returns `result.corrections` with the affected offer IDs and findings. Join those to the catalog by ID. `EMPTY_CATALOG` requires operator review. Under **Completed control**, `READY_FOR_REVIEW` means every expected offer passed the implemented checks. Google makes the final product approval decision.
5. Check `result.access` and `verifiedUnits` separately. `free_compatible_inline` describes a source and size a Free account can submit. A Paid account can also send this input. `paid_feature` identifies a verified authorized URL or a complete audit above 25 offers. `verifiedUnits` is the number of delivered PPE offer events. After a correction, send the regenerated export with a new sequence. Replaying the same sequence suppresses a second Actor start. The imported workflow leaves catalog edits and uploads to the authorized customer process.
